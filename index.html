Instruction-Sheet Skeleton

A lean, local-only skeleton for AI instruction-sheet apps. A public instruction sheet tells your own AI chat how to turn a topic into JSON. The AI hands back an import link, and the app stores the data in your browser. No API keys, no server, no AI inside the app.

Built with Kiro for Kiroween 2026 (Skeleton Crew).

Apps

  • Pack Player

    Import a listening pack and play it back with the browser's built-in text-to-speech.

    Open the app Instruction sheet

    Paste this URL into your AI chat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RazWeb3/instruction-sheet-skeleton/main/instructions/player.md

  • Memory Board

    Import a memory board and browse it as cards, filtered by tag.

    Open the app Instruction sheet

    Paste this URL into your AI chat: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RazWeb3/instruction-sheet-skeleton/main/instructions/board.md
